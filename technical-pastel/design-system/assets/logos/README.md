- `technical-pastel-wordmark-light.svg`: letters in `#eef2f6` (`ink`, dark theme) with the `#6ab3ee` square. For dark grounds.
- `technical-pastel-wordmark-mono.svg`: letters and square both in `#12161c`, for single-ink use only.
- `technical-pastel-app-icon.svg`: app icon and favicon. A `#12161c` tile with a `#eef2f6` "tp" and the `#6ab3ee` square.

Leave clear space equal to the height of the "p" bowl on every side, and keep the wordmark at least 16px tall on screen.
